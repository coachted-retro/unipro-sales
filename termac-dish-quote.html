Key Rental Terms

Equipment Ownership: Termac retains title to all leased equipment at all times. Lessee has no ownership interest.

Authorized Products: Only Termac-supplied chemicals may be used on Termac-owned equipment. Use of any third-party chemicals constitutes a material breach and entitles Termac to immediate repossession without refund.

Payment: Month-to-month, due on the agreed charge date. Card on file required. Payments 10+ days late trigger repossession rights.

Termination: Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. Termac may terminate immediately for cause (unauthorized chemicals, non-payment, tampering).

Repossession: Termac may self-help repossess equipment without court process for any material breach, per Pennsylvania UCC Article 9.
