📋 Recommended Weekly Review Process — Jim Kennedy
Run this protocol at every weekly meeting with Jim & UniPro team
1
Project Manager confirms completion date
PM verifies actual completion on every job listed as Complete or Completed–Not Billed. Completion date locked in system before proceeding.
Owner: Project Manager (Dan Soleau / Ted Scholl)
2
Completion documents and approved change orders sent to billing owner
Signed completion ticket, photos, any customer approvals, and all approved change orders transmitted to billing owner (Catherine Sullivan) same day as completion confirmation.
Owner: Project Manager → Billing Owner (Catherine Sullivan)
3
Invoice issued within 3 business days — 5 days maximum
Accounting issues invoice within 3 days of receiving complete documentation. Any job exceeding 5 days escalates immediately to Jim Kennedy. No exceptions.
Owner: Billing Owner (Catherine Sullivan) → Jim Kennedy for escalation
4
Every unbilled job receives an owner, blocker, and next-action date
No job sits in Completed–Not Billed without a named person responsible, a specific reason it's not billed, and a committed date for the next action. This dashboard tracks all three fields.
Owner: Ted Scholl (field) + Catherine Sullivan (billing)
5
Leadership reviews all red items weekly until billed
Any job in the 6–10 day bucket or above is red. Jim, Dan, and Ted review every red item in the weekly meeting until it is invoiced and removed from the unbilled list. No red item closes without a resolution.
Owner: Jim Kennedy (review lead) — weekly meeting standing agenda item
